Story 2: As a user, I want to be able to Browse and Search the Database for recipes.

2.5 Discover Page (Browse & Search)
Description: A browseable, searchable, and filterable grid of all available recipes.

Functional Requirements:

ID
Requirement
FR-31
The Discover page shall display all available recipes in a responsive grid layout.
FR-32
Each recipe card on the Discover page shall show: emoji/image, recipe name, a short description, cook time, serving count, like count, cuisine badge, and difficulty badge.
FR-33
A text search bar shall filter the displayed recipe grid in real time (client-side) by recipe name and description.
FR-34
A cuisine filter dropdown shall allow filtering recipes by cuisine type (All, Italian, Mexican, American, etc.).
FR-35
Filter "chips" shall allow filtering by difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard).
FR-36
Dietary filter chips shall allow filtering by dietary tag (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
FR-37
Clicking a recipe card shall open a detailed recipe modal showing full ingredients and step-by-step instructions.
FR-38
Each recipe card shall have a like/unlike button that calls POST /api/recipes/{id}/like or DELETE /api/recipes/{id}/like.
FR-39
Each recipe card shall have an "Add to Plan" button that opens a prompt for selecting the day and meal type for the weekly meal plan.
FR-40
A "Create Recipe" button in the Discover page header shall open the recipe creation modal.
